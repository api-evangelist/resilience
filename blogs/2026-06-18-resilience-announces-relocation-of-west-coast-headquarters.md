BLUE ASH, Ohio, June 18, 2026 – Resilience, along with Ohio Governor Mike DeWine, JobsOhio, the City of Blue Ash, and REDI Cincinnati, today announced the relocation of Resilience’s corporate headquarters from San Diego, California to Blue Ash, Ohio, a northern suburb of Cincinnati. Resilience is a leading biomanufacturing contract and development manufacturing organization (CDMO) dedicated to broadening access to complex medicines, with a large drug product manufacturing facility in West Chester, Ohio.
